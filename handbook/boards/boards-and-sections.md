---
title: Boards and sections
description: "Where cards live, how sections group them, and how portals move you between boards."
order: 30
---

A board is a view of the cards a workspace owns. A card placed on a board has a position and a size
there; the same card can be placed on several boards, and editing it anywhere edits it everywhere,
because there is one card and several placements.

A section is a band on a board: a heading, a colour, and a set of cards inside it. Sections are
identities rather than formatting, so a card can be mentioned as belonging to one, the outline can
list them in reading order, and a view can be filtered to "this section" without guessing from
geometry.

A portal is a card that opens another board. A press on it flies through — if the target board is
open in front of you, you go there; if it is not, the portal is the door that opens it. Portals are
cards because a doorway should be placeable, linkable, mentionable and shared like everything else.

Moving between boards is navigation, not tabs. The strip is the list of boards you have visited,
and the back control restores the board you left together with the viewport you left it at.
