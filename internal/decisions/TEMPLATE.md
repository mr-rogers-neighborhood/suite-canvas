# ADR-NNNN: Title

- Status: proposed | partial | accepted | superseded
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Superseded by: link, or delete this line when it is not superseded
- Related: the records, code and docs this decision depends on
- Shipped: what exists today; required when Status is `partial`
- Remaining: what does not exist; required when Status is `partial` or `proposed`

## Decision

What was decided, in two to five sentences. Say what is true, not what is hoped for.

## Rejected alternatives

- What was considered instead, and the fact that rules it out.
- A second one, when the choice was close.

## Consequences

- What becomes easier.
- What becomes harder or is given up.
- What a reader would otherwise get wrong.
