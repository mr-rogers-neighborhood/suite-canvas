---
title: Plans
description: "Why a workspace can be refused, and what a plan has to do with it."
order: 20
---

A workspace is either held by an account or it is local. A plan is what puts one in an account and
keeps it syncing; without one the relay refuses with a reason, and the sync panel names that reason
rather than showing a spinner.

The refusal is not a lock on your data. The local copy stays exactly where it is, the canvas keeps
working, and exporting is always available. What stops is the relay's willingness to hold the
workspace for an account that is not paying for it.

The panel that says this also carries the door: **See plans** opens the account panel on its plan
tab, which is the only place that can fix it. The launcher repeats the same notice beside the
workspaces this browser holds, because a reader who has just paid elsewhere needs the list to
refresh — so it is read again when the tab comes back and when the account panel closes.
