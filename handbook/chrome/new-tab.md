`+` creates a board and opens it. A new board is empty: no sections, no cards, and a name you can
change in place.

Boards are identities, not folders. A card placed on two boards is one card, and editing it on
either shows the edit on both. That is why `+` makes a place to arrange cards rather than a place to
copy them.

If you would rather start from something, the library holds templates and the context menu can
duplicate a board. Neither is on this button: `+` means "a blank board", and a button that
sometimes does something else is a button you learn to avoid.
