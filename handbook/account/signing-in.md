---
title: Signing in
description: "What an account adds, and what a browser keeps without one."
order: 10
---

A workspace works without an account. It lives in this browser, syncs when a relay is reachable, and
exports to a file. Signing in adds the things a browser cannot do: membership in an organization,
invitations, devices, and a licence.

Signing in is not what makes a workspace collaborative. Sharing a board is a link; a relay is a
server. An account is what makes a workspace *yours* across machines, and what lets an operator
answer questions about it.

An account can also be required, at which point an unsigned browser is admitted as local-only and
offered a sign-in. The door is stated rather than assumed: nothing about the canvas changes shape
when you sign in, and nothing is lost when you sign out.

Devices and sessions are listed in the account panel, because "where am I signed in" is a question
people ask after a laptop goes missing.
