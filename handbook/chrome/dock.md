The dock holds two things. The library toggle, which carries the inbox count so "how much is
waiting" is answerable without opening anything, and the button that makes something new.

That second button opens one menu, not a row of buttons: **new card**, **new sketch**, **new
section**, **new portal**, and **type settings**. The same menu appears in the library's *Add new*,
so there is one list of things a workspace can contain rather than two that could drift apart.

Making a card from the dock puts it where you can see it. Making a section or a portal asks for the
board it belongs to, because both are structural: a section is a band on a board, and a portal is a
door to another board.

The dock floats over the bottom-left corner of the canvas and never covers the cards you are
working on for longer than a press; the purpose control, the zoom pill and the gesture control sit
in the same corner and stack above it.
