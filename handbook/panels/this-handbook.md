This is the handbook. It floats over the board, and it is written by the people who make the
product; every article is a Markdown file in the repository, published here and in the
documentation site from the same source.

The list is sorted by what is on screen. A board with a card selected shows the articles about cards
and about selection; the title bar is in the list because it is always there. The count on the
collapsed bubble is how many things on this screen have an article — not how many articles exist,
which would never change.

Resting on an article outlines what it is about and draws a line to it, the same gesture
[hover peek](target:ui:peek-surface) uses. If the thing is not on screen, the line points off it and
the article says so; only opening the article moves the view.

The bubble is also where you tell us something is wrong. **Send feedback** is in the footer, and it
takes a sentence with no category to choose and no form to fill in. When feedback is attached to an
article, the article's title goes with it, so a report about this paragraph does not arrive as an
anonymous complaint about the whole app.

The bubble remembers where you left it, and it dodges the inspector, the dock and any card you have
pinned rather than covering them.
